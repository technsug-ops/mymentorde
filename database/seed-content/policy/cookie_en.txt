1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize and remember certain information – for example, your language preference, login data, or display preferences.

In addition to traditional cookies, we also use similar technologies such as Local Storage and Session Storage. In the following, all of these technologies are uniformly referred to as "cookies".

2. Legal Basis

The use of cookies is based on the following provisions:
- Section 25(1) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act): Consent for non-essential cookies
- Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG: Exception for strictly necessary technical cookies
- Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR: Consent as legal basis for data processing
- Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: Legitimate interest for technically necessary cookies

For all non-essential cookies (in particular analytics cookies), we obtain your explicit consent via our cookie consent banner before they are set.

3. Cookie Categories

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies (always active)

These cookies are essential for the operation of the website and cannot be disabled. They are only set in response to actions you take, such as setting privacy preferences, logging in, or filling out forms.

Legal basis: Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR

3.2 Functional Cookies (with consent)

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as storing display preferences (dark mode, design variant). They are implemented via Local Storage and remain only on your device.

Legal basis: Section 25(1) TDDDG in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR

3.3 Analytics Cookies (with consent)

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our website. They help us understand which pages are most popular and how visitors move around the site.

Legal basis: Section 25(1) TDDDG in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR

3.4 Marketing and Tracking Cookies

We currently DO NOT use any marketing, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. In particular, we do not use Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight, or TikTok Pixel cookies. Should we deploy such cookies in the future, we will update this Cookie Policy and explicitly obtain your consent.

6. Managing Your Cookie Settings

6.1 Cookie Banner

On your first visit to our website, a cookie banner will appear, allowing you to grant or refuse your consent. You have the following options:
- Accept all: You consent to the use of all cookies (including analytics)
- Reject all: Only strictly necessary cookies will be set
- Adjust settings: You select individual categories

6.2 Withdrawal of Consent at Any Time

You can withdraw or adjust your cookie consent at any time with effect for the future. To do so, click on the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of our website.

6.3 Managing Cookies via Your Browser

You can also disable or delete cookies directly in your browser:
- Google Chrome: support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647
- Mozilla Firefox: support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer
- Microsoft Edge: support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge
- Apple Safari: support.apple.com/guide/safari

Note: If you completely disable cookies, some functions of our website (e.g., login, preference storage) may no longer work properly.

7. Cookie Storage Duration

The storage duration of cookies depends on their respective purpose. We generally distinguish between:
- Session cookies: Automatically deleted when you close your browser
- Persistent cookies: Remain after the browser is closed, with individual lifespans (see table in Section 4)

The maximum storage duration of the cookies we use is 14 months (PostHog). Cookies for the cookie consent itself are stored for 12 months so that we can document your consent (proof obligation under Art. 7(1) GDPR).
